# Wanderer Professions 1.0.0

### New
- A calm, visual workbench in place of the game's profession window, which stays one click away. Your professions key opens it.
- Every recipe with its icon, as a list or a wall of icons, with what you can make now in the corner.
- The chosen recipe large: its skill colors with your skill on them, how it binds, where it comes from and how sure that is.
- Reagents as cards: in your bags, missing, in the bank, on another character. A tree of what you can make yourself.
- A progress bar while you craft, a queue you start yourself, and two or three crafts worth doing next.
- Merchant's Favor planner, your characters' recipes and cooldowns, camp objects, Craftsman's Writs, specializations and the next rank.
- An optional guild crafter lookup, off by default.
