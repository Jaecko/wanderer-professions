# Wanderer Professions 1.0.10

### New
- Your gathering professions on the workbench's rail too (herbalism, skinning, fishing), after the others, with their skill: no need to open the game's window to see it. Those Forever gives things to make are read like the others once opened.

### Changed
- The rail draws its professions closer when there are many, so they never cover the pages under them.
- A finer look across the family: one palette for every color, clearer contrast on dark windows and on parchment, the same words in every language.
- The amount to make goes down with a minus and up with a plus, no longer arrows.
- A button opens the profession book again; Shift and a click link the recipe itself; the grid shows the skill colors; up to 999 to make at once.
- Shift+click on the big product icon or a reagent row links it; Ctrl+click tries it on.
- Recipe rows in the list show their tooltip like the icons do; red and grey recipe names are easier to read.
- "Add to the shopping list" and plainer recipe notes.

### Fixed
- A deep reagent tree (sub-crafts of sub-crafts): every line shown, drawn closer, no longer cut after eight.
- A recipe needing a tool or a place (anvil, forge, fire) says it in red and Create waits until it is there.
- The profession book is never opened from the workbench during a fight.
- Guild answers about who can craft a recipe are kept only for the questions you asked.
