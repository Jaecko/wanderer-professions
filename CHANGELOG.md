# Wanderer Professions 1.0.4

### New
- Your gathering professions on the workbench's rail too (herbalism, skinning, fishing), after the others, with their skill: no need to open the game's window to see it. Those Forever gives things to make are read like the others once opened.

### Changed
- The rail draws its professions closer when there are many, so they never cover the pages under them.
- A finer look across the family: one palette for every color, clearer contrast on dark windows and on parchment, the same words in every language.
