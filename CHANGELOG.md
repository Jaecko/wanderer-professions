# Wanderer Professions 1.0.11

### Changed
- The item to enchant's frame closes when another recipe is chosen.
- Enchants: the item to enchant is chosen on the workbench (its square; a click opens a frame of squares as in the bags: what you wear first, gold-framed, then what your bags hold), then laid by the game as from its own window. The amount and the queue step aside for an enchant.
- With the game's look (a choice under Wanderer's Appearance): the Workbench (recipes, headings, the crafter's block) drawn with the game's own parts, its lit and chosen rows, its dividers and colors. Needs Wanderer 1.4.89.

### Fixed
- The item to enchant's frame closes with the workbench and when the profession changes.
