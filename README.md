# Wanderer Professions

A calm, visual workbench for your professions, in place of the game's window (one click away).
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer).

- Your professions on a rail, every recipe with its icon, as a list or a wall of icons.
- The chosen recipe large: its skill colors with your skill on them, where it comes from, how sure that is.
- Reagents as cards: what the bags hold, what is missing, what waits in the bank or on another character.
- Two or three crafts worth doing now, never a long route. A queue you start yourself.
- Merchant's Favor planner, your characters' recipes and cooldowns, camp objects, Writs, specializations before you choose.
- A reagent's own recipes as a tree, and the colors at another skill. Guild crafter lookup, off unless you switch it on.
- Nothing is ever made without a click.
