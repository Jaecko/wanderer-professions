# Wanderer Professions: credits

Wanderer Professions is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (see LICENSE).

Its recipe data (Data/Recipes.lua) is generated from:

- The tables of the WoW Forever game client: the recipes, their reagents, products and skill thresholds.
- The CMaNGOS classic database, https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db (GNU GPL v3): who teaches or sells each
  recipe, what drops it, and where those stand.
- pfQuest by Shagu, https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest (MIT licence): only to tell which zone a place belongs to.
